Offline AI — How to Run It

This drive has an AI chat that works with no internet. It runs on the computer in front of you and opens in your web browser. Nothing is installed.

Start it

  1. In the top folder of the drive, open AI-CHAT:
    • Windows: AI-CHAT.bat
    • Mac: AI-CHAT.command (if blocked: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway)
    • Linux: run bash AI-CHAT.sh in a terminal
  2. Type a number and press Enter:
Number Model Needs Use it for
1 Small — Qwen 3.5 4B about 8 GB of memory Fast answers on almost any laptop
2 Medium — Gemma 4 12B about 16 GB of memory Better, more careful answers
3 / 4 Same, on the graphics card a graphics card (Windows/Linux) Much faster if the computer has a GPU
  1. Wait up to a minute while the model loads from the drive. Your browser then opens at http://localhost:8098.
  2. Type your question. To stop the AI, close the black AI-CHAT window.

On a Mac the graphics chip is used automatically. If option 3 or 4 fails, use 1 or 2.

Ask about your own documents

Click + next to the message box and attach a PDF or text file. The AI reads it and answers from it. See HOW-TO-ASK-YOUR-DOCS.

Two models = a second opinion

The two models come from different makers (Alibaba's Qwen and Google's Gemma). For an important question, ask both and compare. The small model answers at once (its "thinking" is turned off, so it is fast). The medium model "thinks" before it answers; click Reasoning to see its thinking.

Good to know

  • It can be wrong. Check anything safety-critical (medical, electrical, structural) against the reference library (OPEN-LIBRARY) and the books on this drive.
  • The AI knows only what it learned when it was made, plus what you attach. It cannot look things up.
  • Both models are Apache-2.0 licensed. The AI program is llama.cpp (MIT).
