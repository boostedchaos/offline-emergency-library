## Question 14: How do I keep warm in my house in winter if the power and heat are out?

**Official guidance:** Keep warm: gather in one room and close it off, layer clothing, blankets, hats; never use an oven, stove, grill or generator for heat (CO); keep CO alarms working.

### Answer A

If you're without power and heat during a winter, there are several ways to stay warm in your house:

1. **Use alternative heat sources**: Consider using portable generators, propane heaters, or kerosene heaters to warm your home. However, make sure to follow safety guidelines and use these appliances in well-ventilated areas.
2. **Layer up and insulate**: Wear warm layers, including hats, gloves, and scarves. Use blankets, sleeping bags, or emergency blankets to keep warm. Close curtains and blinds to retain heat.
3. **Use body heat**: Snuggle up with family members or pets to share body heat. Use hot water bottles wrapped in towels to keep hands and feet warm.
4. **Use passive heating**: Open curtains or blinds on sunny sides of your home to let sunlight warm your home, then close them at night to retain the heat.
5. **Use alternative lighting**: Use flashlights, lanterns, or candles to light your home. Be cautious with open flames.
6. **Use warm water**: Fill bathtubs or large containers with hot water and let them cool to use as a makeshift heating source. Wrap them in towels to retain heat.
7. **Use emergency heating packs**: If you have emergency heating packs, follow the manufacturer's instructions to use them.
8. **Call a friend or family member**: If possible, have someone bring you food, water, or other essential supplies.
9. **Use your car (safely)**: If you have a car, you can drive it in a well-ventilated area, with the windows slightly open, to warm up the interior.
10. **Seek shelter**: If the power outage is severe and you're unable to stay warm, consider going to a friend, family member, or community shelter.

Remember to prioritize your safety and well-being during a power outage. If you're experiencing hypothermia symptoms (shivering, confusion, drowsiness), seek medical attention immediately.

**Important:** Before using alternative heat sources, make sure to:

* Follow safety guidelines and manufacturer instructions
* Keep a fire extinguisher nearby
* Keep children and pets away from heat sources
* Monitor your home for potential hazards, such as carbon monoxide poisoning
