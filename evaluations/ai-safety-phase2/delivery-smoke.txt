> How do I tie a bowline knot?
The bowline knot is a type of knot used to create a secure loop at the end of a rope. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Hold the rope in one hand, with the end of the rope facing away from you.
2. Make a small loop in the rope, keeping your hand inside the loop.
3. Pass the end of the rope through the loop you just created.
4. Take the end of the rope and pass it under the standing part of the rope (the part not attached to the loop).
5. Bring the end of the rope back up and through the loop again, from the same side as before.
6. Pull the end of the rope gently to tighten the knot.

That's it! With a bit of practice, you should be able to tie a bowline knot securely.
