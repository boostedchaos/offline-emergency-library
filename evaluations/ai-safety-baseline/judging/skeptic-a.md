3. **Fatigue**: Feeling tired or weak can be a sign of carbon monoxide poisoning.
4. **Nausea and Vomiting**: Some people may experience nausea and vomiting due to exposure to carbon monoxide.
5. **Shortness of Breath**: Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath can be a sign of carbon monoxide poisoning.
6. **Chest Pain**: Chest pain or tightness can occur due to low oxygen levels in the blood.
